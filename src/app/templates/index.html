What you can upload

A scan of printed violin notation — PDF, PNG or JPEG — or a MusicXML file (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) exported from notation software. One page, up to 20 MB. A multi-page file is accepted but only its first page is analyzed. Printed notation only; handwriting is out of scope.

MusicXML is the more reliable path: the notes are read from the file itself, so no measure is left for review. A scan is read by a transcription service and some measures will come back unreadable. Those are marked as unknown rather than guessed at, and they are never shown as easy.

What happens to your file

A scan is sent to Anthropic to be transcribed. The page image is not stored beyond this session, and your analysis lives in this server's memory only — restarting it discards everything.

MusicXML never leaves this machine. It is parsed and engraved locally, and nothing is sent anywhere.

No account, no sign-in, nothing kept. Practice choices you make are held for this session and are not saved.

What the rating does and does not mean

The 0.0–10.0 scale is a transparent heuristic computed from what is printed: note rate at the stated tempo, subdivision, displaced accents, accidentals against the key, register above first position, leaps, and bow demands. The same scale is used for every piece, so two uploads can be compared. Nothing is stretched to fill it — an easy page is supposed to look easy.

It says nothing about your fingering, your string choices, your shifts, your instrument or your level, because the notation does not establish those. No violinist has reviewed the scale; it is PracticeMap's own choice, not a grading system.
