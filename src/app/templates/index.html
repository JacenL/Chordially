PracticeMap finds the phrases, estimates how demanding each measure is on a 0.0–10.0 scale, and shows you how to practise the passage you pick.

Your own scan

A scan of printed violin notation (PDF, PNG, JPEG), or a MusicXML file (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) from your notation software.

One page, up to 20 MB. A multi-page file is accepted but only its first page is analyzed. Printed notation only — handwriting is out of scope.

A scan is read by a transcription service, so the page image is sent to Anthropic and is not stored beyond this session. Some measures will come back unreadable, and they are marked rather than guessed. MusicXML is read from the file itself: exact notes, nothing sent anywhere, and no measure left for review.
